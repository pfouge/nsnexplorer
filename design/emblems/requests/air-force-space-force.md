Subject: Request for written permission to display the Air Force Symbol and the Space Force logo on a public reference website

Dear Trademark Licensing Office,

I own and run NSN Explorer (nsnexplorer.com), a free website that organises public U.S. government procurement data (National Stock Numbers, open solicitations and past awards from DLA, SAM.gov and USAspending) so that small and mid-sized American manufacturers and suppliers can find parts and services they are able to provide and compete for them. The site is supported by advertising. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the Department of the Air Force or any other part of the U.S. government, and it says so on every page.

I am writing to ask whether you would grant written permission for the following use, and on what terms.

1. Mark: the Air Force Symbol and the Space Force logo.
2. Where: the site-wide footer only, never in the header, in advertising, on merchandise or in any logo of our own.
3. How: a small mark, about 20 pixels tall, beside the plain-text name of the institution, in a row of institutions whose public buying data the site covers. The row is introduced by a factual line, "Public buying data from", and followed by: "Not affiliated with or endorsed by any U.S. government agency or military service."
4. Artwork: we would like to show all institutions' marks in one colour (dark grey on light backgrounds, light grey on dark backgrounds) so the row reads as one set. Could you tell us whether a one-colour version is permitted, and if so whether you can supply approved one-colour artwork? If only the official colour artwork may be used, we will use it unaltered, or not at all if you prefer.
5. Duration: until either party ends the permission; we will remove the mark within two business days of any request.

If this use is not something your office can approve, a short reply saying so is just as helpful; we will continue to name the Department of the Air Force in plain text with the disclaimer above. If a licence agreement or fee applies, please send the terms and I will review them myself.
Thank you for your time.
